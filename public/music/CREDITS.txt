Music credits for The World Tiki Atlas (tiki.place)
===================================================

SPACE loop (/music/space.m4a)
  Entirely synthesised in code. No samples.

ALOHA loop (/music/aloha.m4a)
  Composition, arrangement and all note data: original, written for this project.

  1. Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont, "FluidR3_GM.sf2", version 3.1
     Copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen <getfrank@gmail.com>
     License: MIT (see the full notice below, reproduced as the license requires)
     Source package: fluid-soundfont_3.1.orig.tar.gz
     Rendered offline with FluidSynth 2.6.1 (LGPL-2.1 library; used as a tool, not redistributed)
     Presets used:

       bank 8 / program 24 "Ukulele"              off-beat "chank" comping
       bank 0 / program 24 "Nylon String Guitar"  syncopated bossa-style comping and two short fills
       bank 0 / program 32 "Acoustic Bass"        two-feel and walking bass

     License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication); https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

       Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Vibraphone/Hard Mallets/Vibes_hard_*_v2|v3_rr*_Main.wav
       Membranophones/Struck Membranophones/Bongos/BongoH_Hit1_v*_rr*, BongoH_HitMuted1_v*_rr*,
         BongoL_Hit1_v*_rr*, BongoL_HitMuted2_v*_rr*, BongoH_Roll_v*_rr*
       Membranophones/Struck Membranophones/Conga/Conga_HitN_v*_rr*, Conga_HitFM_v*_rr*,
         Tumba_HitN_v*_rr*, Quinto_HitN_v*_rr*, Quinto_HitFM1_v*_rr*
       Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Shaker, Small/Mid_ShakerDouble_Down_rr*, Mid_ShakerDouble_Up_rr*,
         Mid_Shaker_Roll_Fast_rr*
       Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Cabasa/Cabasa1_Rub_v*_rr*
       Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Guiro/Guiro_Slow_rr*, Guiro_Fast_rr*
